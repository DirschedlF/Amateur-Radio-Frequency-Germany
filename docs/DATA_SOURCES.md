# Data Sources and Update Process

## Source
- Official: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/afuv_2005/anlage_1.html (Anlage 1 AFuV)
- Version in app: 3. AFuV-Änderung, BGBl. 2024 I Nr. 175 (class N since 24.06.2024)
- Verified against an official printout dated 14.09.2024.

## Known detail
Row 29 (5830-5850 MHz) does not reference Teil B Nr. 13 in the official table, although Nr. 13 lists the range. The app follows the table (no satellite flag on that row).

## Update process
1. Compare the official table with `src/data/anlage1.json` (45 rows) and `teilB.json` (17 entries).
2. Power values: `w` in watt, `type` PEP/ERP/EIRP, `eirp` optional equivalent (EIRP = ERP x 1.64).
3. Update `src/data/meta.js`: `lawVersion`, `checkedOn`.
4. `npm run lint && npm run build`, check in the browser, commit.

## Band plan data (v1.1, extended in v1.1.1)
- Files: `src/data/bandplan.json`, generated by `scripts/gen_bandplan.py` (edit the script, not the JSON).
- Sources (IARU Region 1, text taken from the PDFs with `pdftotext -layout`):
  - HF Band Plan, effective 16 Oct 2020 (edited by DF5JL), `hf_r1_bandplan.pdf`
  - VHF band plan, effective December 2020 (VGC Novi Sad, ON4AVJ), `VHF-Bandplan.pdf`
  - UHF band plan, effective December 2020 (PDF dated 18.03.2021), `UHF-Bandplan.pdf`, covering 70 cm, 23 cm and 13 cm
  - SHF band plan, effective December 2020 (ON4AVJ 02.12.2020), `SHF-Bandplan.pdf`: 9 cm, 6 cm, 3 cm, 1,2 cm (v1.1.1)
  - µWave band plan, effective December 2020 (ON4AVJ 02.12.2020), `µW-Bandplan.pdf`: 6 mm, 4 mm, 2,5 mm, 2 mm, 1,2 mm (v1.1.1)
  - VHF Handbook V10.03, February 2026: used to cross-check 6 m to 13 cm and for additional notes (LoRa on 434 MHz, 144.0025 MHz lower limit, ATV change 2021). The HF, VHF and UHF PDFs from the IARU page were compared by checksum and are unchanged.
- All 148 HF/VHF/UHF segment boundaries (v1.1) and the 45 SHF/µWave boundaries (v1.1.1) were checked programmatically against the PDF text (HF 79, VHF 31, UHF 38). Bandwidth, mode and usage were transcribed by hand and compared with the PDF columns.
- The `cat` field (colour category) is an interpretation by this app for the bar chart only.
- Source quirks kept as printed: HF 3600-3620 and 3600-3650 kHz overlap; the 6 m umbrella row 50.500-52.000 MHz plus its sub rows; UHF row 432.191-432.193 MHz is out of order in the source (flagged in the app).
- When updating, replace the transcription in the script, rerun it, update `BANDPLAN_META` in `src/data/meta.js`.
- Class columns A/E/N in the band plan view are computed from `anlage1.json` (merged ranges, full/partial/none). Power limits and Teil B rules are not part of that check.

## Activity frequencies (hotspots)
- Files: `src/data/hotspots.json`, generated by `scripts/gen_hotspots.py` (edit the script, not the JSON). The script asserts that every frequency lies inside exactly one band from `bandplan.json`.
- Every entry has a source key:
  - `iaru`: centres of activity and calling frequencies from the IARU Region 1 band plans (same transcription as `bandplan.json`).
  - `wsjtx`: FT8, FT4, WSPR, FT2, FST4, FST4W, JT65, JT9, JT4, Q65, MSK144 and Echo (EME echo test) frequencies from the default Working Frequencies table of WSJT-X 3.2.1 (build 261001). Exported with Settings > Frequencies > right click > Save as `.qrg.json` on 12.10.2026 and stored as `scripts/data/wsjtx_3.2.1_frequencies.json` (rows of the modes listed above only). Region 1 view: rows for "All" and "Region 1"; for FT8, FT4 and FT2 only the preferred frequency per band. Type "Digimodes" = FT8, FT4, FT2, WSPR and IARU data frequencies, type "Weak Signal" = the other WSJT-X modes plus meteor scatter.
- Cross-checks: FT8 160 m to 10 m agree with DARC DL-Rundspruch 33/2017 (DF5JL); FT4 10.140 and 14.080 MHz agree with the WSJT-X 2.7.0 User Guide screenshot. FT4 on 40 m is 7.0475 MHz (older beta lists said 7.047).
- Not included: WSPR on 6 m (WSJT-X lists 50.293 MHz for Region 2 and 3 only; the IARU plan has WSPR beacons at 50.401 MHz), FT8/FT4/FT2 alternates that are not preferred (e.g. 7.056, 14.090), FreqCal (calibration stations), JTTY, the QO-100 transponder rows, Region 2 and 3 entries.
- To update: export the table of a newer WSJT-X version the same way (after right click > Reset), replace the file in `scripts/data/`, rerun the script, update `HOTSPOTS_META` in `src/data/meta.js` and the `wsjtx` source text in the script.
